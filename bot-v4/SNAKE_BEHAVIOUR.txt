CODEHAMMER BOT-V3 SNAKE BEHAVIOUR
=================================

bot-v3 uses two explicit strategic phases separated after round 300. Collision,
flood-space, tail-simulation, sonar, portal, and head-order checks remain active
in both phases.

ENGINE SIZE LIMIT
-----------------

The requested early target is length 1 to 2. BattleDragon defines MIN_SIZE as 2
and rejects any action that leaves a dragon shorter than 2. The actual attainable
target is therefore length 2. Length 1 is never requested.

REFERENCE REPLAY: M145346
-------------------------

The replay demonstrates the intended transition:

- round 300: the teams had 64 and 62 living dragons;
- most dragons were length 2 or 3;
- the longest dragons were only length 5 and 6;
- by round 325, the eventual winner had a length-23 leader; and
- at round 500, that team won with longest length 37 versus 34 and total length
  275 versus 162.

Bot-v3 follows the same broad pattern: many minimum-size racers through round
300, followed by zero splitting and strong pearl growth from round 301.

PHASE 1: SMALL-SNAKE SWARM, ROUNDS 1-300
-----------------------------------------

The team target is the engine's full unit limit. Bot-v3 no longer caps itself at
12 racers on large maps.

A dragon becomes eligible to split at length 4. Split sizes remain varied where
the parent is larger, using a deterministic random value in the legal range:

    child minimum = 2
    child maximum = min(5, parent length - 2)

At length 4 the only legal result is exactly 2 + 2. At length 5 the result is
2 + 3 or 3 + 2. Larger parents may create children up to length 5, and those
children later grow and split again until the unit limit is filled.

New processes wait two turns before splitting and parents wait four rounds
between their own splits. This prevents a same-turn cascade while replenishing
lost racers quickly.

Safety requirements before splitting:

- the team must be below the engine unit limit;
- the parent must have at least two fully observed safe moves;
- when the full tail is visible, the child head should have two exits; and
- both parent and child must retain the legal minimum length 2.

Length 6 is the emergency split threshold. At that point an unseen tail is
allowed because the new child receives its own turn and fresh 7x7 vision later
in the same round.

EARLY PEARL AND LENGTH CONTROL
------------------------------

Length-2 dragons value pearls normally so they can reach length 4 and reproduce.
At length 3, pearl attraction falls but remains sufficient to seek the final
growth segment required for a legal 2 + 2 split. At length 4 or greater, pearl
attraction becomes minimal because splitting is preferred.

Once the team reaches the engine unit limit, further splitting is impossible.
Any dragon longer than 2 may then use a fully checked, zero-growth or
negative-growth sprint to spend segments and return toward length 2. It never
sprints below the engine minimum.

PHASE 2: LONGEST-SNAKE GROWTH, ROUNDS 301-500
----------------------------------------------

Starting in round 301:

- splitting stops completely for every role and every length;
- deliberate length-shedding stops;
- a sprint is considered only when it produces at least +1 net length;
- attacker pursuit is suspended so pearls become the shared objective;
- long-range pearl attraction rises to at least 250% of its early value;
- direct pearl value starts at 45,000 and increases by 1,500 per existing
  segment, capped for scoring at length 60; and
- shorter teammates continue yielding to longer teammates through sonar.

The increasing pearl value and length-based right-of-way form positive feedback:
once one dragon becomes longer, nearby shorter teammates give it room and the
leader becomes increasingly committed to collecting the next pearl. Multiple
leaders can grow when they occupy different parts of the map.

The bot does not buy length with a known death. Proven collisions, immediate
dead ends, insufficient flood space, and dangerous head contests still outweigh
all pearl rewards.

EARLY TEAM ROLES
----------------

Through round 300, roles are stable by dragon ID:

    ID divisible by 3  -> ATTACKER
    every other ID     -> SURVIVOR

Attackers of length 4 or more pursue enemy heads, ideally from two to three
tiles away. Length-2 and length-3 attackers temporarily behave safely while
growing toward a split.

Survivors emphasize enemy separation, open exits, two-ply continuation,
flood-filled space, tail access, and teammate right-of-way.

From round 301, both roles suspend these different strategic objectives and
prioritize safe growth. Their physical collision tolerances remain conservative.

COMMON NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY
-----------------------------

1. Known kelp and visible occupied destinations are rejected.
2. Every sprint step and intermediate destination is validated.
3. The visible body is simulated in engine order: collision, head insertion,
   pearl growth, tail release, then sprint cost.
4. Candidate moves are checked for exits, future moves, flood space, tail
   access, visible heads, turn order, and teammate conflicts.
5. A pearl cannot outweigh a confirmed collision or shallow-space trap.
6. Unknown portal exits and invisible destinations remain last-resort moves.

SONAR
-----

Status sonar reports predicted position and capped length. Shorter teammates
yield to longer teammates, which is especially important during the growth
phase. Danger and pearl messages are advisory; current direct vision overrides
them because sonar can be intercepted or imitated.
