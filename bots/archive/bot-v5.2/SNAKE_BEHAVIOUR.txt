CODEHAMMER BOT-V5.2 SNAKE BEHAVIOUR
===================================

bot-v5.2 is a replay-driven hardening of bot-v5. It preserves the existing
two-phase planner, portal memory, sonar protocol, role system, body simulation,
and flood-space checks. The changes address the failure pattern in M173375:

- Team A split only six times while Team B split 105 times.
- every Team A death was a head-to-head trade initiated by Team B;
- six of Team B's seven sprints killed a Team A head; and
- the match ended before round 300, so late-role logic could not rescue it.

ENGINE SIZE LIMIT
-----------------

BattleDragon's legal minimum length is 2. A length-1 dragon cannot be created.
The small-snake swarm therefore uses length-2 racers whenever possible.

PHASE 1: SAFE REPRODUCTIVE SWARM, ROUNDS 1-300
----------------------------------------------

The team attempts to fill the engine unit limit. Length-2 and length-3 dragons
strongly pursue pearls. In particular, a length-3 dragon no longer suppresses
the exact pearl needed to reach split length 4.

A safe length-4 dragon immediately splits into 2 + 2. A larger non-grower
splits off a length-2 child and may repeat on the next round. Splitting is
rejected when:

- the stationary parent head is in confirmed enemy head reach;
- the future child head is in confirmed enemy head reach;
- the child has no visible exit, unless an emergency long-snake split applies;
- the body cannot be reconstructed, except at the emergency length ceiling; or
- the team has reached the unit limit.

Splitting itself is a valid waiting action. The parent is not required to have
a safe movement plan on the same turn, because it does not move during a split
and the shortened parent receives fresh vision next round.

REPRODUCTIVE PEARLS ARE NOT SPRINT FUEL
---------------------------------------

Before the team reaches the unit limit, an ordinary sprint is not selected just
because it crosses several pearls. Even a positive-net sprint wastes segments
that could have produced the next child. Racers walk through food and preserve
all gained length.

A multi-step move remains available when there is no fully safe single-step
move. This emergency exception lets a trapped dragon spend length to escape.
At the unit limit, surplus non-growers may again shed length safely.

DISPERSAL WITHOUT STARVATION
----------------------------

Early dragons still spread across the map through teammate spacing, unseen
territory value, and portal use. While a length-2 or length-3 dragon is still
trying to reproduce, spacing penalties are capped below the value of a pearl.
This prevents two nearby racers from abandoning all food merely to maximize
distance from one another. Occupied destinations remain illegal.

ACTIVE PORTAL USE
-----------------

During phase 1, known paired portals receive a route bonus after the ordinary
collision, occupancy, flood-space, continuation, and head-reach checks. An
unpaired portal remains a last-resort uncertain route.

ENEMY HEAD REACH
----------------

Every turn builds a reach map for each visible head. The search uses:

- the visible or freshly reported lower bound on the dragon's length;
- every currently visible passable edge;
- static visible body occupancy;
- one free ordinary step;
- one segment charged before every additional sprint step; and
- pearls collected on an earlier step as budget for later sprint steps.

This detects the pearl-funded three-step attack observed in M173375. It also
detects a length-2 enemy that first eats a pearl and then pays for a second
step. A pearl on the next destination cannot fund that step because sprint cost
is checked before movement, matching engine order.

The reach map only calls a route confirmed when the complete path is visible
and currently clear. It does not assume that an unseen tile is empty or that a
blocked tail will move in time. Friendly one-step conflicts are treated as
lethal; deliberate multi-step attack reach is reserved for enemies so allied
racers do not paralyse their own swarm.

HARD SAFETY TIER
----------------

A plan is fully safe only when it is:

- known and currently visible;
- not an immediate or shallow-space trap;
- outside every confirmed direct head reach; and
- outside every confirmed enemy sprint reach.

If any fully safe plan exists, unsafe plans are removed from selection rather
than merely receiving a numerical penalty. Pearls, exploration, portals,
dispersal, and attack positioning can never outscore a confirmed head attack.

All other heads get an action before this dragon chooses again: higher IDs act
later in the current round, and lower IDs act earlier next round. Direct and
sprint threats are therefore both treated as potentially lethal. Longer snakes
and locally outnumbered snakes apply the strongest trade-avoidance penalty.

DENSITY-ADAPTIVE SURVIVAL
-------------------------

Safety does not wait until round 300. If at least two enemy heads are visible
and they locally match or outnumber friendly heads, the dragon temporarily:

- disables attacker attraction;
- prioritizes survival scoring;
- increases uncertainty and head-trade penalties; and
- prefers exits, continuation space, and tail access.

This responds to a small-map swarm before encirclement becomes unavoidable.

PHASE 2: SPECIALISTS, ROUNDS 301-500
------------------------------------

The bot-v5 role assignment remains unchanged:

    dragon ID divisible by 4 -> GROWER  (approximately 25%)
    every other dragon       -> SUPPORT (approximately 75%)

Growers stop splitting, pursue pearls with length-scaled rewards, retain their
segments, and receive conservative space and head-safety scoring. Supports
continue the small-snake split cycle, yield to longer teammates through sonar,
and pressure enemy routes without overriding the hard safety tier.

COMMON MOVEMENT RULES
---------------------

1. Known kelp and currently occupied destinations are rejected.
2. Every step and intermediate sprint destination is checked.
3. The body is simulated in engine order: collision, head insertion, pearl
   growth, normal tail release, then the extra sprint segment charge.
4. A pearl gained earlier in a sprint may legally fund a later step.
5. Emergency non-growing sprints are considered only when no safe one-step
   route exists or when surplus length is deliberately being shed.
6. Moves are checked for exits, continuation, flood space, future tail access,
   longer-teammate priority, and confirmed head reach.
7. Unknown portal landings and invisible destinations remain last resorts.

SONAR
-----

The bot-v5 sonar format is retained. Status signals announce predicted position
and capped length for dispersal and right-of-way. Pearl and danger signals keep
their short lifetimes. Direct vision overrides sonar memory because messages
can be intercepted or imitated.
