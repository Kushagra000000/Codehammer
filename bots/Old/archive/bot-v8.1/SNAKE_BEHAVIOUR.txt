CODEHAMMER BOT-V8.1 — PORTAL EXPLORER
=====================================

V8.1 CHANGES
------------

bot-v8.1 starts from bot-v8 and deliberately increases both portal use and
map exploration while preserving the existing collision and kelp rules.

- A paired portal now receives 1,500 score during swarm play instead of 250.
- Growers receive 500 portal score instead of 35, but still require a known,
  threat-free exit whenever a normal safe route exists.
- An unpaired portal loses only 60 score for a swarm snake. Its 1,500 portal
  reward also inherits the surrounding unexplored-territory value, making
  discovery of the unknown exit a likely choice.
- Exploration looks up to eight tiles beyond each candidate. Unseen territory
  receives a strong proximity-weighted reward, and areas not observed for at
  least 18 rounds gradually become exploration targets again.
- Repeatedly visited destinations receive a substantially larger penalty.
- Pearl, portal, and exploration rewards still cannot legalise a kelp edge or
  a visible body collision. Growers retain their threat-free selection tier.

PURPOSE
-------

bot-v8.1 retains the implementation reconstructed from Team B's decisions in:

  - M180685.replay (Trophy, ended on round 214 by eliminating Team A)
  - M180678.replay (Default, completed 500 rounds and won on length)

The replay files were treated only as match data. No source code was available
inside them, so this is an independent behavioural reconstruction rather than
a byte-for-byte copy of Team B's hidden program.

OBSERVED TEAM B PATTERNS
------------------------

1. Team B had zero kelp/wall deaths in both matches. Kelp crossings are
   therefore forbidden at candidate generation time, not discouraged by a
   score.

2. Team B used overwhelmingly single-step movement:

     Trophy:  7,547 single moves, 25 two-step sprints
     Default: 15,565 single moves, 27 two-step and 3 three-step sprints

   Reverse moves were also almost nonexistent. bot-v8.1 evaluates forward,
   left, and right first; reverse is an emergency fallback. Sprints are used
   only for a visible two-step enemy-head strike.

3. Team B formed a minimum-length swarm. On Trophy it grew from 2 snakes to
   62 while keeping the longest snake at length 4. On Default, all four
   initial length-4 snakes split immediately. Of 309 normal Team B splits
   across the two replays, 308 produced a length-2 parent and length-2 child.

4. Team B deliberately accepted head-for-head trades. Trophy ended after 17
   observed Team-B-initiated head trades, and Default contained many more.
   A small non-grower in bot-v8.1 may therefore enter an adjacent enemy head or
   make a two-step head strike. It never deliberately enters an enemy body.

5. Portals were normal routes, not emergencies. Team B crossed the Trophy
   portal 55 times and Default portals more than 500 times. bot-v8.1 remembers
   both edges belonging to each portal ID and rewards portal travel during the
   swarm period.

6. The long Default match changed character after round 300. Team B still had
   54 snakes with a maximum length of 3 at round 300. A minority then began
   retaining pearls while the swarm kept splitting. In the final quarter the
   population contracted and surviving snakes grew; the final four lengths
   were 12, 11, 6, and 18 after the last event.

PHASES
------

ROUNDS 1-300 — UNIFORM SWARM

- Every dragon tries to remain length 2.
- At length 4 or more, and while a team slot exists, it leaves a length-2
  parent and splits all remaining tail segments into a child.
- Movement favours safe unexplored space, moderate pearl progress, portals,
  and separation from teammates.
- Length-2 and length-3 dragons may trade against an enemy head.

ROUNDS 301-425 — SPECIALISATION

- A stable, mixed hash of the dragon ID selects approximately 25% as growers.
- Growers stop splitting, value pearls more strongly, and will not volunteer
  for head trades.
- The remaining snakes keep the small-snake split cycle and contest enemy
  heads. This preserves the replay's simultaneous growth and swarm pressure.

ROUNDS 426-500 — FINAL GROWTH

- Every surviving dragon stops splitting and retains pearls.
- Known head threats, one-exit cells, shallow reachable space, stale pearl
  targets, and teammate right-of-way receive conservative weights.
- A threat-free candidate forms a hard selection tier whenever one exists.

MOVEMENT SAFETY
---------------

Each possible move is processed in this order:

1. Reject a known kelp edge.
2. Resolve a remembered portal pair; an unpaired portal is permitted only as
   an uncertain swarm route and is heavily penalised for a grower.
3. Reject every visible occupied destination except a deliberate small-snake
   enemy-head trade.
4. Predict one-step destinations of all visible heads.
5. Count exits and flood reachable space from the destination.
6. Score pearl progress, exploration, portal travel, forward continuity,
   teammate separation, enemy spacing, and deterministic per-turn variation.
7. When retaining length, choose only from fully known, threat-free,
   non-trapping moves if at least one exists.

SONAR AND RIGHT-OF-WAY
----------------------

Every action sends a 64-bit status signal in all four directions. The signal
contains:

  - a bot-v8.1-compatible signature and team bit;
  - predicted head position;
  - predicted length;
  - round and dragon ID; and
  - whether the sender is currently retaining length.

Fresh friendly signals are used as moving exclusion zones. Short snakes yield
extra space to longer teammates, while all snakes avoid converging on the same
head position. Team and signature fields prevent ordinary enemy sonar values
from being mistaken for friendly right-of-way messages.

NON-GOALS
---------

- bot-v8.1 does not deliberately omit an action to reduce the team population.
  The no-valid-action deaths seen in M180678 were correlated with congestion
  and were not treated as proof of intentional suicide.
- It does not reproduce Team B's exact random generator or hidden memory. The
  deterministic jitter recreates its varied left/right choices without making
  tests nondeterministic.
- It does not copy bot-v5, bot-v5.1, bot-v5.2, bot-v6, or bot-v7 movement code.
  Only the unchanged official protocol helper is carried into this directory.
