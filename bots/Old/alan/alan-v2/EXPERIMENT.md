# alan-v2 phase-timing experiment

Parent: `bots/current/bot-v10.3.3`

Hypothesis: map-aware gather and sacrifice deadlines, plus population-ready
specialisation, would improve late-game conversion without tactical changes.

Decision: rejected. The 30-game, all-map, both-sides evaluation against the
parent finished 15-15 with no errors. It swept Autarky and Trauma, but lost
both sides on Slithery Fight and Stronghold. Map dimensions alone did not
predict the time the team needed to gather.
